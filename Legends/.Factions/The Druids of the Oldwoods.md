# The Druids of the Oldwoods

## From Legends
Deep within the *Oldwoods* is a druid grove ringed by monolithic stones. The druids of *Oldwoods* are white clad men and women who wear laurels and wield oaken staves. They worship the mother goddess **Thylea**, and believe that the people of *Mytros* should never have settled in her *Heartlands*. They perform a daily ritual sacrifice to **Thylea**, hoping to atone for the sins of the mortal species.
