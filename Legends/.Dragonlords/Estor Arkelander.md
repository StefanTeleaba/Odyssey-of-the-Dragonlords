# Estor Arkelander

## From Legends
- **Estor Arkelander** was the oathsworn of **Yralune**. He was a terrifying man who slaughtered the armies of the Titans indiscriminately. He sought immortality and was ultimately tricked by **Lutheria**, who cursed him to roam the oceans forever as a ghost. **King Acastus** of *Mytros* is the last surviving Arkelander.

## From his Tomb at the *Necropolis of Telamok*
- **Estor Arkelander** was the oathsworn of **Kyrah**.
