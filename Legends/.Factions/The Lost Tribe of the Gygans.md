# The Lost Tribe of the Gygans

## From Legends
The gygans were **Sydon**’s favored people, six armed giants with a single eye. Once the greatest civilization in *Thylea*, the Gygan Empire fell during the First War. The ruins of this empire can be found on countless islands throughout the *Cerulean Gulf*. The few surviving gygans keep to themselves, but take any opportunity for vengeance against the hated settler species.
