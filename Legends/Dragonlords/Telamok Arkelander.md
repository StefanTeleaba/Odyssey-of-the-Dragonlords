# Telamok Arkelander

## From Legends
- **Telamok** was the oathsworn of **Vakylux**. He was **Estor**’s brother, though he did not follow his brother into the *Nether Sea* on his final journey. Instead, he joined **Adonis** in the last stand against **Sydon**, where the two of them were killed. Whether he has any living descendants is unknown.
