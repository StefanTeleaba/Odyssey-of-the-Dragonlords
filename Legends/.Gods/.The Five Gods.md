# The Five Gods

## From Legends
The Five Gods are widely celebrated as the settlers’ favored gods. With the exception of **Mytros**, they are not heaven-dwelling deities but familiar faces who walk among their people. They have been known to take mortal husbands and wives, rule over cities, and sire demigod children. They helped the Dragonlords and the mortal species to defeat **Sydon** and **Lutheria** during the First War. For centuries, they have blessed the mortal species, protecting them from tyrannical domination by **Sydon** and **Lutheria**.  
Shrines to the Five gods can be found in nearly every settler household. Prayers to the Five must be accompanied by the sign against the evil eye to ward off the jealous gaze of **Sydon** and **Lutheria**.
