# The Raving Ones

## From Legends
The Raving Ones are a den of fey creatures who worship **Lutheria**. On the edges of the *Oldwoods*, west of *Atrokos*, there is a system of caves where they are said to dwell. They sometimes travel along the roads in the guise of maidens in distress, luring travelers back to the den to be torn apart and devoured in rituals to the goddess of revelry. The Raving Ones are the mothers of the goatlings, who are born from the union of a satyr and a Maenad.
