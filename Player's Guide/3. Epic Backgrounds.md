# Chapter 3: Epic Backgrounds

> **Epic Paths Reimagined**  
*In the original Odyssey of the Dragonlords, the epic backgrounds were presented as epic paths. This option remains available by replacing the ability scores, feats, skills, tool proficiencies, and starting equipment of an epic background with those of a standard background. Alternatively, Epic Paths have been reimagined as backgrounds and may be used as such.*  
*These epic backgrounds work identically to the original epic paths except that they are now not combined with an additional background. You will see that the epic backgrounds listed in the next section have all of the features that a typical background would have.*

Epic backgrounds are an optional addition to character creation. One of the recurring themes of ancient Greek myths is that many heroes are “born of the gods.” Epic backgrounds are designed to give players mythic archetypes for their heroes, so that they feel like divinely touched figures, such as Hercules, Achilles, Odysseus, or Theseus.  
Each player should choose one of the epic backgrounds described in this section. This will determine their hero’s mythic context within the land of Thylea and influence relationships with her gods and people. Epic backgrounds are different from standard backgrounds in that they are specific to this setting. They unlock new aspects of the adventure, including special relationships with story characters and access to specific magic items.  
Every epic background has a set of goals. While these goals are personal to each hero, they are supplementary to the overall goals of the adventure. When a hero accomplishes their goals, they become blessed by the gods. Each epic background has a different divine blessing that is granted upon completion of its goals.  
There are also magic items listed under the goals for each of the epic backgrounds. Some items may be located in distant parts of Thylea and must be found before they can be used. Other items may be forged or built, and a hero must discover how to build the item, find the necessary components, and then find a forge where the work can be done. Heroes often have a choice between several magical items.  
In addition to linking each hero to the divine powers of Thylea, epic backgrounds are intended to help tie the heroes to the story in a powerful way. When you think of the greatest fantasy epics, the heroes always have strong ties to the story—special relationships with kings, queens, villains, and gods. The heroes of these stories are important for reasons that go much deeper than the powers and the items they wield.  
Epic backgrounds should not diminish player agency. Players are free to pursue the story hooks presented in each background in any way that the DM deems appropriate. They are also free to modify an epic background with the agreement of the DM. The DM should take care when modifying the magic items, however, as the adventure is balanced with these specific items in mind.

## Cursed One
**Ability Scores:** Charisma, Intelligence, Wisdom  
**Feat:** Skilled  
**Skill Proficiencies:** Perception, Survival  
**Tool Proficiencies:** Calligrapher’s Supplies  
**Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) Calligrapher’s Supplies, Healer’s Kit, Fine Clothes, 14 GP; or (B) 50 GP.

You come from a great family or tribe that has been cursed for centuries, owing to the actions of one of your distant ancestors, who angered the Titans. Whatever the nature of your curse, the stigma has haunted you for all your life. Wherever you go, you are shunned by the ignorant and the superstitious. As a result, you have felt like an outsider for all your life. You know that as long as you remain cursed, you will be incapable of living a full life. Moreover, you are not sure that your family or your tribe will survive for another generation unless you find a way
to lift the curse.

*For players who love:* Cadmus, Atreus (Greek Tragedies)  
*Restrictions:* None.  
*Adventure Hook:* The Oracle reveals that she knows who can end the curse.

**Heroic Tasks**  
- To end your family’s curse.
- To find the Gem of Brightness. Only you can attune.
This is a family heirloom.
- To find a greater family artifact that is key to ending
the curse. Pick one magic item from this list:

***Staff of Fire:*** Can be any melee weapon.  
***Belt of Fire Giant Strength:*** This belt also grants resistance
to fire.  
***Efreeti Bottle:*** Only you can take a Magic action to use it.

**Divine Blessing**  
***Blessing of the Gods.*** You and your direct family are immune
to curses, diseases, and the Poisoned condition.

## Monstrous Species
Automata, satyrs, centaurs, jancan, gygans, minotaurs, medusae, myrmekes, nyxian, and sirens are considered monstrous species. They have unique heritages that require slight changes to each epic background. Please refer to the Monstrous Species Epic Backgrounds table.

**Table Legend**  
***A: Adopted Kin.*** You were adopted by a family, but a dragon destroyed them.  
***B: Borrowed Tribe.*** You were welcomed by a tribe that later vanished without a trace.  
***C: Chosen Calling.*** Founding a Dragon Order has become your purpose.  
***D: Displaced Native.*** You were a champion of a native tribe defeated by the Dragonlords. You seek to rebuild the order for all peoples.  
***E: Excluded Species.*** This epic background does not work well with this species.  
***F: Forbidden Subclass.*** Dragon Knight or Oath of the Dragonlord should not choose these backgrounds.  
***G: God Touched.*** You are descended from Pythor’s many unions with mortals and native races. You represent Oldwoods druids or the steppe tribes.  
***H: Highly Compatible.*** This epic background is a strong match for this species.  
***K: Kinsworn Chieftain.*** You led a mixed or monstrous tribe of the distant steppes before it mysteriously vanished.  
***L: Living Vessel.*** Pythor and a willing woman gave fragments of their souls to seed your automatos creation, making them your parents.  
***M: Myrmekes Curse.*** Your entire species is cursed. Your memories are broken, but you recall volunteering to seek the Oracle and learn how to end it.  
***O: Outsider Myrmekes.*** The crew of a pirate ship became your family. You sailed to Thylea seeking treasure but also hoping to find your true homeland.  
***P: Pirate Brassguard.*** You are a brassguard construct who captained a ship from beyond the Forgotten Sea, crewed entirely by others of your kind.  
***S: Stranger.*** You came from across the Forgotten Sea and do not recognize the history or customs of your species in Thylea, for your people followed different traditions.  
***T: Titan Bloodline.*** One of your ancestors was a Titan, such as Sydon or Lutheria.
