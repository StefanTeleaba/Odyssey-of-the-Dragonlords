# Adonis Neurdagon

## From Legends
- **Adonis** was the oathsworn of **Thorpyrax**. He was a wealthy, handsome, brave, and generous man who is widely celebrated in the legends. But his overconfidence eventually got him killed. His dynasty survives through **Taran Neurdagon** in the city of *Mytros*.

## From his Tomb at the *Necropolis of Telamok*
- **Adonis** was the richest fo the Dragonlords.
